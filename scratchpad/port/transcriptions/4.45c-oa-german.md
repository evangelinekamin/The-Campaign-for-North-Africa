Verbatim transcription of the German OA Chart from the 1979 SPI rulebook
*The Campaign for North Africa*.

**Source scan:** `tmp/The Campaign for North Africa.pdf`, rendered at 200 DPI.
**PDF pages rendered and read for this transcription:** 159, 160, 161, **162, 163,
164, 165, 166**, 167 (159-161 = the Italian chart that precedes it; 167 = the

## Chart header text (PDF p.162), verbatim

> This chart divides the German units into groupings termed "sheets." For example,
> the Sonderverband 288 Regiment sheet lists all of the counters used in *CNA* to
> represent the forces historically assigned to the Sonderverband 288 Kampfgruppe.
> This chart includes all of the units colored and specified on the counter sheet as
> Germans. The following German terms appear in the OA Chart.

**German-term glossary (verbatim):**

- Panzer = Armor
- Machinegewehr = Machinegun
- Panzerjaeger = Anti-tank
- Flak = Anti-air
- Aufklarungs = Reconnaissance
- Pioneer = Engineer
- Schutzen = Motorized Infantry
- Kradschutzen = Motorcycle infantry
- Gebergs = Mountain infantry
- PnzrGrndr (PanzerGrenadier) = "Armored" infantry
- SchwInf = Schwertze-Infanteriegeschutzen (Heavy Weapons)

Other abbreviations appearing in the rows (standard): Bn = Battalion, Bde = Brigade,
Regt = Regiment, Bty = Battery, Koy/Coy = Kompanie/Company, HQ = Headquarters,
SP = Self-Propelled, Lt = Light, Med = Medium, AA = Anti-Aircraft, Pzjg1 =
Panzerjaeger I (SP AT gun).

## How to read this transcription

**Columns** (rule 4.44): `Notes | Unit | Counter Abbreviation | ID Code | TOE & Weapon System(s) | Arrives`.

- **ID Code** indexes the German Unit Characteristics Chart (a separate chart; not
  part of this transcription). Codes are lowercase (a … gg).
- **TOE & Weapon System(s):** `N` = arrives at full/normal TOE; `U@x` = understrength,
  arrives with `x` TOE strength points; `O@x` = overstrength, arrives with `x`. A list
  like `4×PzII, 8×PzIIIH, 2×PzIVE` = the individual TOE strength points the counter
  arrives with.
- **Arrives = `OpStage/Game-Turn`** (rule 4.44: "The Operation Stage/Game-Turn on
  which the unit arrives"). OpStage ∈ {1,2,3}; Game-Turn ∈ {1…111}. `D` = already
  deployed at scenario start (no German unit uses `D`). Rule 4.44 states this column is
  **"a check on the Reinforcement Schedule"** — see FLAG 1.
- **DEPTH = the assignment depth (the whole point of this chart, rule 4.44).** A unit
  is assigned to the nearest row **above** it whose depth is exactly one less. Depth is
  shown two ways for zero ambiguity: an integer `Depth` column, and `—` prefixes on the
  Unit name (`—` = depth 1, `——` = depth 2, `———` = depth 3). Depth 0 = a formation top
  (an HQ) or a wholly independent unit.

Every depth level below was verified by cropping and magnifying the unit-name column
of the 200-DPI render (left-edge alignment), not by eyeballing the full page.

**Notes (verbatim):**

> **a =** In the second Operations Stage of the Novemeber IV, 1941 Game-Turn (2/58),

> 1941. The Axis Player is provided with both counters but may only replace one with

(*"Novemeber" is the book's spelling.* TOE of the 21st Panzer Div HQ is literally
printed as "(b)", i.e. "see note b".)

> The 15th Panzer Division is by far the most powerful unit in the game. Adequately
> supplied and used with skill it is virtually unstoppable. It reflects the marvelous
> use the Germans made of the divisional organization. It is a lesson in combined arms

## Sheet 8 — Kampfgruppen HQ's

**Basic Morale: That of the attached units**
**ID Code: b**

This sheet has **no unit rows** — the Kampfgruppen are formed ad hoc from battlegroup
counters. It is a rules-and-notes sheet.

**Notes (verbatim):**

> **a =** Kampfgruppen were *ad hoc* formations (brigade-level) of virtually every type
> and combination of units. They were formed for specific missions, and reflected the
> incredible German administrative/organizational flexibility. German units rarely
> stayed in division formation in Africa, and the use of Kampfgruppen was prevalent.
> The Kampfgruppen headquarters listed below are a random selection of actual groups
> formed during the campaign: Gruppe Badde, Gruppe Bach (*Bach*), Gruppe Dedeking,
> Kampfgruppe Fischer, Kampfgruppe Graf (*Graf*), Kampfgruppe Kaiser, Kampfgruppe Knabe
> (*Knabe*), Kampfgruppe Kost, Gruppe Marcks (*Marcks*), Kampfgruppe Menny, Kampfgruppe
> Mitte, Kampfgruppe Pfeiffer (*Pfeiffer*), Gruppe Schwerin (*Schwrn*), and Gruppe Voss.
> These units may be formed and disbanded at will, within the restrictions listed below.
> If the Axis Player wishes to form more Kampfgruppen that he has battlegroup counters,
> he may do so.

Numbered restrictions printed to the right (verbatim):

> **1.** A Kampfgruppe is formed simply by attaching a German unit to it, thereby
> creating the unit.
>
> **2.** The Kampfgruppe counter must be removed (disbanded) when detaching the final
> German unit (elimination through combat does not apply). In such a situation any
> remaining Italian units must be detached and any remaining anti-tank TOE Strength
> Points assigned to the headquarters become replacement points.
>
> **3.** The German Player may never attach/assign more than two artillery-*type* units
> to a Kampfgruppe unless it is either the 1st or 2nd Afrika Artillery Regiment
> headquarters (see the German non-Divisional artillery units sheet).
>
> **4.** No more than a total of three Italian units may be attached to all of the
> German Kampfgruppen in play at any time.

## Flags — proxies, judgement calls, and contradictions

**FLAG 1 — [4.43b] vs [4.45c] arrival-turn contradiction (ALREADY RULED; do not
re-litigate).** Rule 4.44 states the Arrives column "is a check on the Reinforcement
Schedule," i.e. [4.45c] is a cross-check, not the authority, for *when* a unit shows up.
Where the OA-chart Arrives values disagree with the [4.43b] Reinforcement Schedule
already seeded in the engine — in particular the 5th Light Panzer Division's panzer
timing (this chart: 5th Light Div HQ 3/21, 5th Panzer Regt HQ 2/21, I/5 1/22, II/5
2/22) — **[4.43b] wins** per the standing owner ruling ("[4.43b] Reinforcement Schedule
wins the 5-Panzer arrival contradiction over the [4.45c] OA chart"). This chart is the
authority for **parent structure / assignment depth / morale / ID / TOE**, not for
arrival timing. Noted, not resolved.

**FLAG 2 — 90th Light, II/155 Schutzen Bn ID Code printed "K" (uppercase).** I/155 and
III/155 read lowercase "k"; the Unit Characteristics Chart uses lowercase codes only
(a…gg). This is near-certainly a typographic misprint for "k". Transcribed as printed
("K"); the implementer should treat it as "k". Judgement call — flagged, not silently
normalized.

**FLAG 3 — 164th Light, 165th Aufklarungs Bn counter abbreviation printed "164"
(not "165").** The unit's historical name is 165th, but its counter number is 164
(matching its parent division). This may be intentional (recon bn bearing the division
number) or a misprint. Transcribed exactly as printed (Unit "165th Aufklarungs Bn",
Counter "164"). Flagged for the implementer to reconcile against the physical
counter-sheet if/when the counter art is consulted.

**FLAG 4 — Non-Divisional Artillery, I/722 Artillery Bty counter erratum.** The book's
own footnote states the counter abbreviation "I/772" (as printed on the physical
counter) is incorrect; the chart's abbreviation I/722 is the correct one. Transcribed
the chart value (I/722) and preserved the erratum footnote. This is the book flagging
its **own counter-sheet misprint** — analogous to the recorded 54.17 errata pattern.

**FLAG 5 — Non-Divisional Artillery / ArKo 104 is a soft grouping, not a hard tree
(proxy caution).** Note b explicitly says the four battalions indented under ArKo 104
(149, 362, 408, 528) were merely "at one point assigned" and that ArKo 104 caps at five
artillery units. If the seeder hard-wires those four as permanent children of ArKo 104
it will over-constrain them; the faithful reading is a loose Kampfgruppe-style artillery
HQ. Notes a/b/(Sheet-8 restriction 3) govern how many units these artillery HQs may
actually hold. Judgement call surfaced, not resolved.

**FLAG 6 — II/155 Artillery Bn (Non-Divisional) intentionally blank.** Its TOE and
Arrives are blank because the counter *is* the 5th Light's I/75 Artillery Bn, materializing
only on the conditional swap in note e (and Sheet-1 note d). Transcribed blank, not
guessed. Any seeder must model it as a conditional re-label of I/75, never as an
independently-arriving counter (note d: "may not create a II/155 from replacement
points").

**Cross-sheet reminders (not contradictions):** several counters legitimately migrate
between sheets — the 104th Schutzen Regt (Sheet 3) reassigns to the 21st Panzer (Sheet
1, note c); the 155th Artillery Regt (Sheet 9) can attach to the 5/21 Panzer; the
707/708 SchwInf Koy (Sheet 7) attach to the 164th (Sheet 5, note a); III/255 & III/347
(Sheet 11) later join the 200th PanzerGrenadier Regt. Counter numbers also repeat
across unit *types* (e.g. "33" = 33rd Panzerjaeger / Artillery / Aufklarungs / Pioneer;
"155" = 90th's Schutzen regt vs the Non-Divisional Artillery regt) — these are distinct
counters, disambiguated by unit name + ID code, not a transcription error.
