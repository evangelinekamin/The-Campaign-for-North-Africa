## How indentation was read (this is the load-bearing datum)

Per rule 4.44 (docs/rules/04-game-equipment.md:130) and the p.147 column-explanation box, a unit's
**parent formation is encoded by how far its name is indented** in the "Unit" column:

- **d=0** — name **not indented**: the unit has **no parent formation** (the sheet's Div/HQ counter,
  or a stand-alone unit).
- **d=1** — indented **two spaces**: parent = **the nearest d=0 unit above it** on the sheet
  (normally the Division HQ). These are the regiment/brigade HQs *and* the divisional support units.
- **d=2** — indented **four spaces**: **two parents** — its brigade/regiment-equivalent parent is
  **the nearest d=1 unit above it**, and its division-equivalent parent is the division HQ counter.

Indentation was not eyeballed. Each sheet's "Unit" column was measured by leftmost-clean-ink per text
row (dither/stipple removed by a 3×3 neighbour filter); every division/CCNN sheet resolved into three
clean clusters ≈26 px apart (d0 / d1 / d2). Two sheets whose auto-measurement was corrupted by dense
gray stipple (**17th Pavia**, p.150) or by Notes-column morale parentheticals (**Unassigned Infantry
& Recon**, p.158) were settled by high-zoom visual crops with a pixel grid; both conform to the
standard template. Single-row stipple glitches on **142nd Inf Regt HQ** (Catanzaro) and **7th
Bersaglieri Regt HQ / 46th Artillery Regt** (Trento) were overridden to their structurally-obvious d=1
(they are a regiment HQ and divisional artillery, respectively).

In the tables below the **`d` column is the authoritative depth**; the leading `·`/`··` on the Unit
name mirrors it for readability.

## [4.44b] header text (verbatim, p.147)

> This chart divides the Italian units into groupings termed "sheets." For example, the 16th "Pistoia"
> Division sheet lists all of the counters used in *CNA* to represent the forces historically assigned
> to the 16th Semi-motorized Infantry Division.
>
> The following sheets represent something of a compromise between game and simulation. There were a
> great many unassigned and *ad hoc* Italian units; certainly the number of artillery units — as
> compared to the opposition, and even those of the Germans — is almost staggering. Likewise, many of
> these units were captured in entirety or completely destroyed. Records for these units are scarce and
> somewhat contradictory. We have attempted to provide every unit we could get accurate research on, on
> a battalion-regiment level. To that end many units have been "melded" into others, for the sake of
> simplicity and, to be honest, the parameters of the counter-mix. For example, all the Italian 81mm
> mortar companies have been abstracted into other artillery units or headquarters units' values.
>
> Each nation's Order of Battle had its own peculiar difficulties; the Italian's was confusion and
> profusion. Of the three OB's for the game, it is probably the least accurate historically. This group
> includes all of the units colored and specified on the counter sheet as Italians.

### Column-heading explanation (verbatim, p.147)

> **Formation:** The grouping of units in this sheet. **Basic Morale:** The rating for all of the units
> listed on this sheet. **Unit Assignment:** This is indicated by the position of a unit't [*sic*] name
> in the "Unit" column. A unit whose name is not indented does not possess a parent formation. A unit
> whose name is indented two spaces possesses a parent formation. Its parent unit is the first unit
> above it on the sheet whose name is not indented. A unit whose name is indented four spaces possesses
> two parent units. The brigade-equivalent parent unit is the first unit above it whose name is indented
> two spaces. Its division-equivalent parent unit is the division headquarters counter. …
> **SIZE-DESIGNATION** (actual name, not its size equivalent): **Div** = Division; **Regt** = Regiment;
> **Bn** = Battalion; **Coy or Koy** = Company; **Bty** = Battery. **TYPE: HQ^E** = Headquarters with
> engineering capability; **HQ** = Headquarters without engineering capability; **Arty** = Artillery;
> **AT** = Anti-tank; **AA** = Anti-air; **Inf** = Infantry; **MG** = Machinegun; **Mtr** = Motorized;
> **Mitrg** = Motorcycle; **Heavy Wpns** = Heavy Weapons; **SchwInf** (Schwertze-Infanteriegeschutzen)
> = Heavy Weapons. **OTHER: CCNN** = the abbreviation for Camicie Nera (Italian for 'Blackshirt');
> **RECo** = Raggruppamento Esplorante Companie (reconnaissance company); **Gaf S di C** = Guardia alla
> Fronteria, Settore di Coperature (roughly "Garrison Troops") — Europeans living in Libya "dragooned"
> for the occasion, poor troops at best; **CD** = Coastal Defense.
>
> **TOE & Weapon System(s):** number and type of TOE Strength Points the unit possesses on
> arrival/deployment. **N** = maximum TOE Strength Points for that ID Code. **U@x** = Understrength at
> 'x' TOE Strength Points. **O@x** = Overstrength at 'x'. Tank/gun TOE Strength Points are always listed
> by number of each Weapons System (e.g., "6 65/17 Gun & 4 75/18 Gun-Howitzer").
> **Arrives:** the game-turn the unit is placed on the map. **D** = Deployed (begins the first Operations
> Stage of Game-Turn One on the map). **`#/#` = OperationsStage / Game-Turn** (e.g., `1/18` = OpStage 1
> of GT 18; `2/58` = OpStage 2 of GT 58). "This column is a check on the Reinforcement Schedule."

**Italian abbreviations gloss (from the sheets):** Fanteria/Fanterie = Infantry; Corazzata = Armored;
Motorizzate = Motorized; Articelere = "Fast" artillery; Artiglieri/Artigliera = Artillery;
Ragruppamento = grouping (administrative); Bersaglieri = elite light infantry; Mitrg (here) = mitraglieri
(machinegun) company; Legion (Legione) = a CCNN/Blackshirt brigade-equivalent; RECo/RECAM =
reconnaissance grouping; PAI = Polizia dell'Africa Italiana; Guastatori = assault engineers.

  Battalions.

**Book/print observations (recorded, not resolved):**
1. **Header number [4.44b] vs [4.45c]** — see the flag at the top of this file. Filed as [4.45b].
2. **"Sermovente" / "Semovente"** — the book prints the Ariete note-d spelling as "Sermovente" and the
   Littorio TOE as "Semovente." Transcribed verbatim per-occurrence; both mean the Semovente 75/18 SP
   gun.
3. **Bardia "30th Gaf S di C HQ" counter abbreviation prints "32GaF"** — the row is unambiguously the
   *30th* Gaf HQ (heads I/30, II/30, III/30), but the counter-abbrev cell reads "32GaF". Transcribed as
   printed; likely a counter-abbrev typo for "30GaF". Not resolved.
4. **1st CCNN "133rd Infantry Bn"** — the 1st CCNN's 219th Legion contains a battalion numbered "133".
   This is a Blackshirt infantry battalion, distinct from the 133rd "Littorio" Armored Division and from
   the III/133 Fanterie Bn on p.158. Same number, different units — transcribed as printed.
5. **RECAM HQ TOE "1×vv"** — the HQ's own TOE is printed as "1×vv" (i.e., one TOE point of ID-code "vv",
   the RECo/armored-car type). Transcribed as printed.
6. **ID-code column blanks** — tank battalions attached as whole counters (I(L)/II(L) on Ariete &
   Unassigned Armored, IX(L), II(M), I(M), the CCNN attachments, San Giorgio) print no ID code / TOE on
   some sheets because their stats live on their *home* sheet or the Unit Characteristics Chart.
   Blanks preserved.

**Method/faithfulness notes:**
- Indent depth was pixel-measured, not eyeballed (see "How indentation was read"). Every division and
  CCNN sheet resolved into clean 3-level clusters; the two problem sheets (Pavia, Unassigned Infantry)
  were confirmed visually. This matters because the whole purpose of this chart is the parent structure
  that lets divisions form and [15.53] Organization-Size fire.
- All ~30 sheets on pp.147-161 are captured. Sheets with no HQ counter (garrisons, unassigned/ad-hoc,
  3rd CCNN) are marked; their d=0 rows are top-level with no division parent.
- TOE, ID codes, counter abbreviations, and Arrives values are transcribed as printed from the 200-dpi
  renders. A handful of very small ID-code glyphs on dense gray sheets (e.g., the single/double-letter
  codes b/bb/bbb, h/j, t/u/v, x) are legible but low-contrast; if any downstream cell looks wrong,
  re-render that page at ≥300 dpi to confirm. None looked ambiguous enough to flag individually beyond
  item 3 above.
