## 1. Rule 22.3 FACILITY REPAIRS, verbatim (docs/rules/22-repair.md, scan p.035 — read
against the render, zero discrepancy against the existing OCR transcription)

> **[22.31]** There are two types of Repair Facilities: Temporary Facilities and Major
> Facilities. Temporary Facilities may be constructed by the Players (see Case 24.8), and
> those in existence at the start of a scenario are designated in that scenario. **Major
> Repair Facilities are found in Tripoli (the Tripoli Box), Tobruk (for whoever controls
> it), Alexandria and Cairo (all hexes).** The effectiveness of Repair facilities may be
> reduced or neutralized by bombing (see Cases 22.35 and 39.37) and Artillery Barrage.
>
> **[22.32]** If a Broken down Vehicle or Destroyed Tank begins a Maintenance Segment in a
> hex with a Repair Facility and that vehicle has not been Towed during that Operations
> Stage such vehicle(s) may undergo Repairs.
>
> **[22.33]** Players Repair by type of vehicle: Truck Points, Recce/AC Points, Brokendown
> Tanks and Guns by Type, and/or Destroyed Tanks by Type.
>
> **[22.34]** For each type of Brokendown vehicle the Player rolls one die and consults the
> Broken Down Vehicle Repair Table (22.8) under the correct column (Temporary or Major
> Facility). **The dieroll may be modified by the effects of bombing on the Fortification
> Level of the city** (see Case 22.35 [sic — this cross-reference is the rule's own,
> and it is wrong: the bombing-modifier case is 22.34a below, not 22.35, which is the
> supply-cost case; not corrected, transcribed as printed]). **The result of the dieroll
> is the percentage of that type of vehicle that may be Repaired** (see Case 22.25 for
> details). See Case 22.4 for Destroyed Tanks.
>
> **a.** If the Repair Facility is in a Major City and the Enemy Player succeeds in
> reducing the Fortification Level of that City by **one** (see Air Bombardment Table) add
> **one** to the dieroll. If the level is reduced by **two or three** add **two** to the
> dieroll (whether reduced by Air Bombardment and/or Barrage).
>
> **b.** If the Repair Facility is not in a Major City and the Enemy Player delivers enough
> Air Bombs or Artillery Barrage that, when/if using Air Bombardment Table, to reduce a
> Fortification one Level, that Repair Facility is neutralized for that OpStage. It may not
> repair any vehicles.
>
> **[22.35]** For each Truck Point, Gun or Tank TOE Strength Point undergoing repair the
> Player must have present in that hex and expend (before rolling for Repairs) **one Store
> Point and one Fuel Point**. He may attempt to repair only those points he has expended
> supplies for.
>
> **[22.36]** Temporary Repair Facilities may be affected by Weather (see Case 22.13d).
> **Major Facilities are never affected by Weather.**
>
> **[22.37]** If a **Temporary** Repair Facility is in the ZOC of an Enemy unit it may not
> be used for Repairs unless it is in a Major City. **Major Repair Facilities may be used
> when in Enemy ZOC's.**
>
> **[22.38]** See Case 24.8 for how to construct a Temporary Facility.

Scan-verified word-for-word against `docs/rules/22-repair.md` — no discrepancy in the rule
prose itself. `22.32`'s "Maintenance Segment" is the book's own name for the moment repair
is checked (not a separate engine phase — the existing `Phase.REPAIR` beat, `engine.py`,
is where the whole Repair Phase, field and facility alike, already fires per 22.12).

## 2. Rule 24.8 CONSTRUCTING REPAIR FACILITIES, verbatim (docs/rules/24-construction.md,
scan p.037 — zero discrepancy against the existing OCR transcription)

> **[24.81]** Only temporary repair facilities may be constructed as new; all major repair
> facilities are already in existence (see Case 22.31). Both temporary and major repair
> facilities may have their effectiveness reduced or neutralized by air bombardment and/or
> artillery barrage. Temporary facilities may be destroyed by the presence of Enemy units;
> major facilities may not be so destroyed.
>
> **[24.82]** All Engineer units — including HQ's — may build, rebuild or dismantle
> temporary repair facilities or rebuild major facilities.
>
> **[24.83]** Construction of a new temporary repair facility entails the presence of one
> Engineer unit of any size for **three Construction Segments**, along with the expenditure
> of **250 Store Points and 150 Fuel Points** (most of which represent machinery,
> lubricants, etc.). All supplies must be present at the start of construction and are
> expended immediately.
>
> **[24.84]** Repair facilities in major cities that have had their City Levels reduced by
> bombardment (see Case 22.35) may be rebuilt by first rebuilding the Level of the city (see
> Case 24.4) and then expending **50 Stores Points and 30 Fuel Points**, along with the
> presence of an Engineer unit spending **one** Construction Segment in the hex.
>
> **[24.85]** A temporary repair facility may be built in any major city hex or in any
> village hex. However, no Player may ever have more than **two** temporary repair
> facilities at any one time.
>
> **[24.86]** Temporary repair facilities may be dismantled. It takes one unit with
> Engineering capability one Construction Segment to dismantle a temporary repair facility.
> When the facility is dismantled, the Player has **120 Store Points and 25 Fuel Points**
> in that hex. The dismantled facility exists no more, and if a new one is to be built, it
> must be started from scratch.
>
> **[24.87]** Temporary repair facilities may not be Constructed in an Enemy-controlled
> hex. However, all Repair Facilities may be rebuilt or dismantled in an Enemy-controlled
> hex.
>
> **[24.88]** A temporary repair facility that is in the process of being dismantled may
> not be used.

Already in `data/breakdown_rates.json::broken_down_vehicle_repair_22_8.by_die`, columns 3-4
(`facility_temporary`/`facility_major`) — confirmed exact match against the p.103 render,
digit for digit, including the `10%*` single-TOE-exception cells.

**Structural fact that matters for the port, and differs from Field Repair:** 22.34's own
text — "the result of the dieroll is the **percentage** of that type of vehicle that may be
Repaired" — makes the Facility columns a **percentage applied uniformly to whichever class
is being repaired** (truck, AC/Recce, or tank/SPA alike), unlike the three separate Field
columns (Field Truck and Field AC/R are **flat point counts**, only Field Tank/SPA/TD is a
percentage). A broken-down Truck Point repairing at a facility is therefore NOT the
"roll 1 → 2 points, roll 2 → 1 point" of 22.23 — it is "roll X → that percentage of the
hex's broken trucks," with the 22.25 round-up-fractions and single-TOE-10%-exception rules
applying exactly as they do for tanks. Confirmed by the chart's own row labels: the Facility
columns are two single columns (Temporary, Major), not three per-class columns the way Field
is.

## 5. The 22.34a die-modifier footnote — OWNER RULING (chart's shorthand vs the rule's own
prose; resolved here in favour of the prose, matching house convention)

Scan p.103 footnote, verbatim: *"Add one to the die roll if the Facility is in a Major City
hex whose Fortification Level is **at present** two. Add two to the die roll if attempting
repairs at a repair facility in a Major City hex whose Fortification Level is **at present**
one or zero."*

Rule 22.34a's own prose (scan p.035), verbatim: *"If the Repair Facility is in a Major City
and the Enemy Player succeeds in **reducing** the Fortification Level of that City by
**one**... add one to the dieroll. If the level is **reduced by two or three** add two to
the dieroll."*

**These two statements of the same rule are only equivalent for a Level-3 Major City**
(Alexandria/Cairo, `[25.12]`/`data/city_forts.json`): reduced-by-1 from 3 lands on 2 (chart:
"at present two" → +1, matches); reduced-by-2-or-3 from 3 lands on 1 or 0 (chart: "at
present one or zero" → +2, matches). **They diverge for every Level-2 Major City** (Tobruk,
Bardia, Benghazi, Helwan): the chart's literal words would add +1 to an **undamaged**
Level-2 city's repair die (current level 2 = "at present two"), which contradicts 22.34a's
own conditional ("if the Enemy Player **succeeds in reducing**...") — an undamaged facility
that has never been bombed cannot be penalised by a rule that only fires on a successful
reduction.

**Ruling applied here: the numbered rule's prose wins** (same convention as §3 above and
`fort_buildable`'s established precedent). `game.repair.facility_die_modifier` computes the
modifier from **how far the hex's fort_level has fallen below its own static/printed
baseline** (`state.terrain.fortifications[hx] - state.fort_level(hx)`), which generalises
correctly to a Level-2 city (reduced by 1 → +1, by 2 → +2) and collapses to exactly the
chart's own numbers for a Level-3 city, so nothing about Alexandria/Cairo's modifier changes
under either reading — only Tobruk/Bardia/Benghazi/Helwan's would, and only while damaged.

## 10. THE REVIEW REPAIR (2026-07-31) — four rule defects the adversarial review found in
the slice above, all four fixed, plus the two findings refused and why

Scan re-read for this pass at 200dpi, whole pages, read by eye: **034** (22.13a and 22.22
in full), **035** (22.23–22.37), **102** (the `[22.15]` Vehicle Repair Supply Costs Chart),
**103** (the `[22.8]` table and its die-modifier footnote). Renders under
`scratchpad/22_3_repair/`.

**1. THE FIELD FALLBACK WAS MISSING — the slice repaired LESS than the book allows.**
`engine._repair` routed every broken vehicle/truck on a Major Facility hex into the Facility
path with no alternative, and that path returned without repairing when 22.35's cost could
not be paid. Verbatim, p.034–035: **22.32** — "such vehicle(s) **may** undergo Repairs";
**22.22** — "Field Repairs may take place in **any** hex in which there is a Broken down
vehicle stacked with a Friendly unit of *any* type", which excludes no Facility hex; and the
`[22.15]` chart's `Field | Bd | Truck, AC, Recce` row is **None** (22.23/22.24 repair those
free). So a Player standing on a drained Tobruk still holds the free 22.23 die by right.
FIXED: the two facility helpers now report whether an attempt was made, and `_repair` falls
back to the Field column when it was not. MEASURED on the shipped code: 4 of 34 facility
truck-repair calls per benchmark, 209 broken Truck Points, previously repaired nothing.

**2. 22.13a's MAJOR-FACILITY EXCEPTION WAS DROPPED.** p.034, verbatim: "If the vehicles to be
Repaired are in an Enemy-controlled hex, regardless of the presence of Friendly units in the
hex. **The only exception to this is if the vehicles are in a Major Repair Facility, in which
case the presence of an Enemy Zone of Control has no effect**"; doubled by 22.37 ("Major
Repair Facilities may be used when in Enemy ZOC's") and by 22.13d's own "This does not apply
to Major Repair Facilities". The slice applied the control gate to the Facility path
identically to the Field one, and its new test *commented* that as the rule. FIXED, and the
test restated to assert the printed exception. Recorded honestly in code: this engine's
`state.control_of` is territorial last-sole-occupier control, not the book's ZOC-controlled
hex (10.0 — "Hexes upon which a unit exerts a ZOC are called controlled hexes"), i.e. a
STRICTER proxy than what 22.13a exempts; the exemption is applied to the proxy because the
proxy is what stands in for 22.13a here. Measured INERT on both benchmarks (zero facility
repairs ever occur in an enemy-controlled hex on either log).

**3. THE `[22.15]` COSTS WERE LITERALS BESIDE THEIR OWN TRANSCRIBED CHART.**
`data/breakdown_rates.json::vehicle_repair_supply_costs_22_15` carried the whole chart and

`tests/test_repair.py::test_repair_supply_costs_match_the_22_15_chart` binds every row,

### REFUSED, with the reason

**6 (the tank half). "One die per TYPE" cannot be ported for tanks and is NOT approximated by
inventing a type.** 22.25 rolls "once for the PzII's and once for the PzIV Specials" in a
hex. This engine carries no per-counter tank type at play time: `data/unit_stats.json`'s
per-model ratings are resolved at BUILD time (`game.oob._make_unit`) and the model NAME is
never kept on the counter, and outside ten Italian records in
`data/oob_campaign_extra.json` no OOB record names a model at all — the type comes from
`oob.MODEL_DEFAULTS`, one default per (nationality, role). Pooling the repair die on that
default would put every German panzer battalion in a hex under ONE die, merging exactly the
types 22.25 separates: a larger error in the opposite direction, resting on a build-time
placeholder rather than on anything the order of battle prints. One counter is one battalion
of one type, so a die per counter never merges two types; it only splits a type that fields
two battalions in one hex. Refused on the same ground as the Rosetta terrain override (do not
make data assert something the source does not) and the "no Axis HQ-Engineering" refusal (do
not invent a counter or an attribute for symmetry). **The AC/Recce half of the same finding is
NOT a proxy problem and was fixed**: 22.24 prints "one die for **all** the A/C's and Recce
points in the hex" with no type subdivision, and 22.33 lists "Recce/AC Points" as one
