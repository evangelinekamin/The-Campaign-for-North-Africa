# 30.5 NAVAL TRANSPORT OF TROOPS — transcription and an HONEST NEGATIVE

**VERDICT: DO NOT BUILD. Deferred with three independent structural gates, each sufficient on its
own.** Measured over **5 full 111-turn campaigns (1,665 Operations Stages)** plus an independent
re-measurement, before a line of code was written.

Scan: PDF **page 42 = book folio 42** (middle column, heading through 30.54; right column, 30.54 tail
through [30.6]; [31.0] ROMMEL begins below). Renders at 150/300/400/600/1200 dpi in
`scratchpad/30.5-scope/`. Full write-up: `scratchpad/30.5-scope/30.5-transcription.md`.

**Citation caution recorded for future slices:** PDF index == book folio only for pp.1–52. PDF p.74
prints folio 23 (second booklet); PDF p.96 = chart folio 1; PDF p.110 = chart folio 15.

## The OCR is verbatim faithful — the BOOK is what is defective

`docs/rules/30-the-mediterranean-fleet-commonwealth.md:82-106` is **word-for-word exact across all
nine cases, 30.51–30.59, zero textual errors.** Divergences are formatting only (lost italics, [30.6]
not promoted to a heading, column-break splits). Four defects are the book's own:

1. **`Case 00.00` is what the book prints** — an unfilled 1979 editorial placeholder, confirmed at
   1200 dpi. Same page: [30.23] "the Derna hex (00000)", [30.15] "hexrow xx29". **This corrects an
   error in this port's own 56.3 transcription**, which had claimed the OCR misread it and that the
   scan "read 56.3 at 250dpi, unambiguous". See the correction block there. [56.3] is the right
   *inference* — chapter 30's logistics references run **five sections low** ([30.0] cites "51.0" for
   the Axis Convoy Rules = 56.0; [30.58] cites "50.2" for blocking harbours = 55.2; air references run
   +2, "39.3" = [41.3]) — but it is an inference for a **named errata key**, never the book's text.
2. **[30.55] inverts embarkation and debarkation**: "must **start** the Operations Stage in the port
   of **debarkation** … transferred to the port of **embarkation**, where it is **landed**." Harmless
   only because [30.54] takes the **smaller** of the two ports "be it the port of embarkation or
   debarkation", so `min(origin.out, dest.in)` is safe under either reading.
3. **[30.59]'s "Port Capacity Chart" does not exist.** The book's own Charts index carries no 30.x
   entry at all (it jumps 29.7 → 35.23). The referent is **[55.3]**, chart folio 15.
4. **[30.57]'s percentage has no source.** [55.3] has four columns — Efficiency Level, Stacking Points
   In, Out, Maximum Tonnage — and **no percentage column anywhere**; 25% is not derivable from any
   cell (Benghazi's Efficiency is 3, not 4). Whether the 25% in 30.57's own Benghazi example is
   universal or Benghazi-specific is **undecidable from the book: an owner ruling.**

## An owner ruling CLOSED from the scan, not deferred

[6.3] CAPABILITY POINT EXPENDITURE SUMMARY (PDF p.96, chart folio 1, 300 dpi) prints
`Railroad or inter port transport of troops | 0`, against [30.55]'s "a cost in Capability Points
equivalent to its Capability Point Allowance". **The chart's own footnote reconciles them:** *"certain
actions, while not requiring the expenditure of capability points, restrict a unit's ability to expend
Capability Points."* So **[30.55] is EXHAUSTION, not expenditure** — the same notion [8.73] uses for
rail. That is transcribed, not a judgement call.

## [30.56] — the THIRD control notion, and why it should not become a fourth dict

> "Transfer may take place between any Friendly-controlled Ports. **(Control means the last Player to
> occupy the hex.)**"

It is the rule's only legality gate. It is **not** 54.41's pass-through control (that clause is *pass
through*; a unit marching across claims under 54.41 and not under 30.56), and it is not the engine's
`GameState.control` in three specific ways: `_record_control` moves only on **sole** occupancy; it
counts **`is_combat` only**, where 30.56 says "Player"; and it is sampled once per Operations Stage in
the RECORD phase, while a 30.5 transfer would run in the Truck Convoy Movement Phase **earlier in the
same stage** — a one-Operations-Stage lag, which is the divergence most likely to bite (a port taken
this stage reads enemy-held exactly when you want to reinforce it).

**Recommendation if 30.5 is ever built: gate on `control_of(port.hex)` and NAME the three divergences
in code.** Do not add a fourth control dict — a new invariant surface and a signature move for a
narrow difference — and do not silently reuse `control` while calling it 30.56.

## *** WHY IT IS DEFERRED: THREE STRUCTURAL GATES, EACH SUFFICIENT ***

30.5 needs **two friendly ports**. Measured, seeds 1941/7/4/11/23, full 111 turns, ports counted
friendly by hex control (ports serve the controller, not the flag) and required to pass [30.58]:

```
cw_usable_hist: {1: 333}      cw_2plus: 0      cw_ports_seen: {PORT-Matruh: 333}
```

**The Commonwealth has exactly ONE usable port in 333 of 333 Operations Stages and never two —
~2,000 Operations Stages with zero opportunities for a single transfer.**

1. **The campaign seeds only THREE `Port` objects**: PORT-Benghazi (Axis, 3/3), PORT-Matruh (Allied,
   1/1) and PORT-Tobruk (Axis, **eff 2 of max 5**). **Alexandria — [55.3]'s largest Commonwealth
   personnel port, In 5 / Out 10 — has no `Port` object at all**, deliberately: the bottomless
   Cairo/Alexandria MAJOR_CITY base needs none. Seeding it is a change to the **supply** model, not a
   30.5 change.
2. **Tobruk is Commonwealth-held in 0 of 1,665 Operations Stages**, and so are Bardia, Benghazi and
   Derna. The historical case 30.5 exists for — a besieged Tobruk resupplied by sea — **never occurs
   in this engine's campaign**, because every seed is the known upstream Axis rout.
3. **[30.58] SHUTS TOBRUK BY THE RULEBOOK even in the counterfactual.** PORT-Tobruk is efficiency 2 of
   max 5 = **40%**, against 30.58's threshold of **2/3 (67%)**. Granting the Commonwealth the port
   would still not open the rule.

**And a CW-held port WAS land-isolated in 16 of 1,665 Operations Stages (~1.0%) — every one of them
Sollum, whose [55.3] "In" is 0 stacking points.** All 328 Commonwealth combat units are 1 stacking
point ([9.4]: Battalion 1, Company 0), so in every one of those 16 stages **30.5 could not have landed
a single combat unit.**

**The one route that ever exists** is Alexandria → Mersa Matruh, ceiling `min(Alex out 10, Matruh in
1)` = **one battalion per Operations Stage** — and it runs parallel to the Alexandria–Mersa Matruh
railway, which **[8.72] already authorises for personnel** under near-identical preconditions. So
30.5's only reachable route duplicates an unbuilt rail rule on the same two hexes.

## What IS missing, recorded so the debt has a name

`grep -rn "30\.5[0-9]\?" game/ data/ tests/ scripts/` → **zero hits**; not one of the nine cases
exists. It is missing at the deepest level: **the engine has no primitive for moving a unit except
land movement** — three `replace(u, hex=...)` sites in `apply.py`, all land. There is no Policy hook
for personnel-by-sea, no event kind, and no stacking-point billing anywhere.

**Transcribed already and read by NOTHING** (grepped, zero consumers) — this is the ninth instance of
the pattern:
- `ports_55_3.efficiency_and_stacking` — the In/Out **stacking-point** schedule that *is* [30.54]'s
  "Port Personnel Capacity"; the [55.3] legend says so outright.
- `equivalent_weights_54_5.stacking_point_equivalents_by_rail_interport` — Truck/Motorization 1/10,
  Replacement Point 1/5, SGSU 1/2, **Unit of Stacking Points 1/2**. "Interport" is the charts' own
  name for the 30.5 mechanism; its tonnage column reads `n/a`, so **rail and sea personnel movement
  are not rated in tons at all**.
- **`Port.kind`** — 30.5 would be [55.11]'s first consumer.

## A REAL OCR DEFECT found en route, in a different file

`docs/rules/90-charts-tables-and-play-aids.md:1565-1577` **drops the entire Maximum Tonnage column**
from [55.3] and mangles the header. Not live debt — `data/logistics_rates.json` carries it correctly

from `docs/` would get a four-column table as three. (The legend's "San Girogio" misspelling is the
book's own, not OCR.)

## The condition under which this should be revisited

30.5 becomes live the moment **the Commonwealth holds a second port** — which in this campaign means
the moment the Axis rout ends. That is Gate C's question, not a port question. Build it then, against
a board where it can fire, rather than now against one where it provably cannot.
