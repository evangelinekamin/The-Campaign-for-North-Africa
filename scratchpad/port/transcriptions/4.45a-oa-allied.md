## Column headings (as printed on the chart, page 116)

Transcription rows below preserve the print columns in this order:

`Notes | Unit | Counter Abbreviation | ID Code | TOE & Weapon System(s) | Arrives`

Chart's own definitions (verbatim sense; page 116 header block):

- **Formation:** the grouping of units in this sheet.
- **Basic Morale:** the rating for all units listed on this sheet. A number in parenthesis indicates
  the (untrained) Morale Rating all the units arrive/deploy with.
- **Unit Assignment (indentation):** indicated by the position of a unit's name in the "Unit" column.
  - A name **not indented** possesses no parent unit.
  - A name **indented two spaces** possesses a parent unit = the first unit above it whose name is not
    indented.
  - A name **indented four spaces** possesses **two** parent units: its brigade-equivalent parent is
    the first unit above it indented two spaces; its division-equivalent parent is the division
    headquarters counter.
  - (In this transcription indentation is reproduced with nested bullets: level 0 = flush,
    level 1 = one indent, level 2 = two indents.)
- **Counter Abbr.:** the type as it appears on the counter's unit identification line.
- **ID Code:** letter key ('a' thru 'uu') into the Commonwealth Unit Characteristics Chart (4.46a).
- **TOE & Weapon System(s):** number and type of TOE Strength Points on arrival/deployment.
  `N` = normal (max TOE for that ID Code); `U@#` = understrength at # TOE SP; `O@#` = overstrength at
  # TOE SP; tank/artillery/anti-tank SP listed by count x type.
- **Arrives:** date the unit is placed on the map, in CNA game terms. `D` = Deployed (begins the first
  Operation Stage of Game-Turn One on the map). `#/-` = the Operation Stage; `-/#` = the Game-Turn.
  The printed form is **OpStage/Game-Turn** (e.g. `3/58` = OpStage 3 of Game-Turn 58).

Abbreviations defined on the chart: NATIONALITY: Aus = Australian; NZ = New Zealand; SA = South
African. (Indian Rajput units are NOT an abbreviation for Rajputana units.) SIZE: Div = Division;
Bde = Brigade; Regt = Regiment; Bn = Battalion; Coy = Company. TYPE: HQ^E = Headquarters with
engineering capability; HQ = Headquarters without engineering capability; Arty = Artillery;
AA = Anti-air; Inf = Infantry; MG = Machinegun. OTHER: Ryl = Royal; Rfls = Rifles.

**Intro text (page 116, verbatim sense):** "This chart subdivides the commonwealth units into
groupings termed 'sheets'. Example: the 1st Armored Division sheet lists all of the counters used in
CNA to represent the forces historically assigned to the 1st Armored Division. An order of battle for
the Commonwealth (i.e., British, Indian, Australian, New Zealand, etc.) forces is a frustrating
objective at best. The Allies switched unit assignments so often that it is almost impossible to
follow the location and assignment of a particular unit with any accuracy, especially in game terms.
Most affected were armor and Indian formations, which changed units regularly... The Commonwealth
Player may choose to maintain the original assignments or may choose to switch the units around in a
haphazard fashion (however, see Section 19), as did the actual Allied commanders. The choice is his,
we simply provide the units."

## Selby Force / The Matruh Garrison  — *(page 121)*
**Basic Morale: None (that of attached units). ID Code: a**  *(prose-only sheet — an HQ counter, no
roster of assigned units)*

This is a brigade-level headquarters unit that is present mostly for administrative purposes. It is the
Mersa Matruh Garrison HQ at the start of the campaign for a collection of infantry battalions that will
later become part of other, formal brigade formations. The counter is deployed on **1/1** but may not
be voluntarily moved until it becomes the Selby Force or the Operations Stage in which it is attacked in
a Close Assault. **No units may be assigned, although up to six units may be attached** (see the Maximum
Attachments Chart) and these are considered garrison units for Mersa Matruh. On Operations Stage 2 of
Game-Turn 12 (**2/12**), the unit becomes the **Selby Force** (the only Commonwealth Battle Group). It
may be moved freely, its maximum attachments change, and it is no longer a garrison unit. It is
disbanded during the Organization Phase of **3/21**; remove the counter from play and detach all
attached units. Historically, Selby Force consisted of the 3rd Coldstream Guards, one troop of the 7th
Hussars, elements of the 1st Durham Light Infantry, one company of the 1st South Staffordshires, W
Company of the 1st Royal Northumberland Fusiliers, one company of the 1st Cheshire MG Battalion, the 8th
Field Regiment Artillery plus elements of the 107th Royal Horse Artillery.

## 22nd Guards Brigade (200th Guards Brigade)  — *(page 121)*
**Basic Morale: None (that of attached units). ID Code: a**  *(prose-only sheet — a brigade HQ counter

The 22nd Guards Brigade is an infantry brigade HQ with no battalions initially assigned. However, if it
is attached to an armored division, it possesses the attributes of a Motor Brigade Group and may conform
to that time period's formation organization. The 22nd Guards Brigade is famous — or perhaps infamous —
for the number of identification changes it underwent during the campaign. Essentially formed in
February 1941 (**3/21**) as an infantry brigade HQ (with a standard complement of three infantry
battalions) it underwent the following name changes:
- In January 1942, it became the 200th Guards Brigade.
- In April 1942, that became the 200th Guards Motor Brigade Group.
- In May 1942, that became the 201st Guards Motor Brigade Group.

Units that were assigned at various times to the formation include the 2nd Scots Guards, the 1st Durham
Light Infantry, the 3rd Coldstream Guards, the 1st Buffs, the 9th Rifle Brigade, various anti-tank
companies. The 22nd Guards was assigned to the 70th Infantry Division from February 1941 (2/21) until
April 1941 (1/29). **Note that there are two counters (`22 Gds` and `200 Gds`) provided for this unit.**

## "Arrives" format & cross-check with the [4.43a] Commonwealth Reinforcement Schedule
The OA "Arrives" column is printed **OpStage/Game-Turn** (e.g. `3/58` = OpStage 3 of GT 58; `D` =
Deployed at start; the book: "This column is a check on the Reinforcement Schedule"). The [4.43a]
Commonwealth Land Unit Reinforcement Schedule (PDF p.114-115) lists the same arrivals with **GT** and
**OpS** in separate columns. Spot-checks confirm they agree, e.g.:
- 1st Armored Div HQ = OA `3/56`  ↔  [4.43a] "56 / 3: HQ 1st Armored Div".
- 2nd Armored Bde HQ + 12th Lancers Bn = OA `3/58`  ↔  [4.43a] "58 / 3: 2nd Armored Bde …, 12th Lancers Bn".
- 2nd Armored Div HQ = OA `1/15`  ↔  [4.43a] "15 / 1: 2nd Armored Div".

No Commonwealth OA-vs-[4.43a] contradiction was found in spot-checks. A full row-by-row reconciliation
against the seeded reinforcement data belongs to the implementation block, not this transcription.
(The standing "[4.43b] wins" ruling concerns the *German* OA/5-Panzer contradiction and does not apply
to any Commonwealth sheet here.)

   Artillery" is labelled **`dd`** in the note-list, but the note letter beside the 3rd SA Field
   Artillery Regt (THA) row prints as a single **`d`**.
5. **Indentation resolutions (indentation IS the parent structure — the point of this chart):** verified
   by pixel left-edge detection on the unshaded 2nd Armored Division and by zoom elsewhere —
   32nd Army Tank Bde HQ = L1 (not L2); Kings Dragoon Guards = L2; 11th Hussars (7th Armored) = L1;

6. **Prose-only HQ sheets (no unit roster):** *Selby Force / Matruh Garrison* and *22nd Guards Brigade*

   transcribed in full. The *13th DCO Lancers* prints blank ID/TOE on the 18th Indian Brigade sheet

7. **Superscripts:** `HQ^E` = HQ with engineering capability (the only engineering-HQ type the chart
   defines; every division/large-formation HQ so marked). Where a scan superscript was ambiguous it was
   read as `HQ^E`.
8. **Untrained morale:** parenthesised morale numbers are the untrained/arrival morale (e.g. 1st Armored
   Div `+2 (+1)`; 1st SA Inf Div `0 (−3)`). Two sheets put a **note letter** in the morale parens
