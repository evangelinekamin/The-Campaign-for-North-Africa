## Cases (54.4–54.46), transcribed verbatim, PDF page 74

> **[54.4]** Under certain conditions the Axis Player may make use of the coastal railline extending
> from Egypt to Libya (the Commonwealth railroad system). In essence, he must control the rail hexes
> and he must import rolling stock. Remember, the Barce railroad east of Benghazi is not usable
> under any conditions.
>
> **[54.41]** At any time the Axis player controls five or more contiguous rail hexes he may use such
> rail hexes to transport equipment and personnel. To control a rail hex the Axis player must be the
> last player to have a land combat unit of any type pass through that hex.
>
> **[54.42]** The Axis Player may never build new rail line. However, he may repair rail hexes he
> controls at the same rate as the Commonwealth Player.
>
> **[54.43]** To make use of controlled Commonwealth Raillines the Axis Player must convoy Rolling
> Stock (locomotives, tenders, stock cars, flat cars, etc.) from Europe to Africa. For each 250
> Stores and 100 Fuel Points that the Axis Player imports to Africa and brings to any controlled and
> operative rail hex he may activate all such hexes under his control (as long as they are
> contiguous) to the extent of hauling 300 Tons of Supplies in any one direction during an
> Operations Stage. (Rail movement occurs in the Convoy Stage).
>
> **[54.44]** In order to transport one Stacking Point of any type of unit the Axis Player must have
> active enough Rolling Stock to transport the equivalent of 900 Tons of Supplies in any one
> direction.
>
> **[54.45]** Once Stores and Fuel is applied as Rolling Stock the points are used up; they may not
> be recovered. If at any time the Axis Player loses control of enough rail hexes so that he does not
> have the necessary five contiguous hexes the Rolling Stock is considered to have been destroyed.
> The Axis may not use Commonwealth Stock, nor vice versa.
>
> **[54.46]** All rules concerning the movement of troops/supplies and the use of the railroad that
> apply to the Commonwealth apply equally to the Axis.

No die is rolled anywhere in 54.4 — like 56.3, it is fully deterministic.

## What [54.46] pulls across (the Commonwealth rail rules, same page)

* **[54.33]** "The railroad may transport only one type of supply at a given time. It may move fuel,
  ammunition, or stores — not any combination of the three. (Water need not be transported by RR —
  the railroad hexes are pipelines in and of themselves.)"
* **[54.34]** "For the duration of *one* Operations Stage per month (calendar month), the railroad
  may not be used for anything. It is transporting water forward for railroad use. Players must
  state each month which Operations Stage they are not using the railroad."
* **[54.35]** "Like personnel, supplies may be moved from any one spot and dumped in another spot.
  Supplies are considered unloaded when they reach a specific hex. They may not be moved that
  Operations Stage."
* **[54.31]** The railroad "may move either supplies or personnel — it cannot move both at the same
  time."
* **[54.32]** gives the COMMONWEALTH 1,500 tons per Operations Stage in either direction. The Axis's
  300 tons per activation (54.43) is a fifth of that per unit of rolling stock — the asymmetry is the
  book's, and it is the point of the rule.

## *** THE FINDING THAT REORDERED THIS SLICE ***

**[54.41] DEFINES ITS OWN CONTROL NOTION, AND THE ENGINE DID NOT HAVE IT.** "To control a rail hex
the Axis player must be the last player to have a land combat unit of any type **pass through** that
hex" is:

* **claimed by TRANSIT** — a unit that crosses a rail hex and marches on still takes it; and
* **STICKY** — it survives the unit leaving, and is only lost when the other side crosses in turn.

`GameState.control`, which `engine._record_control` maintains, is neither: it is derived from **sole
combat OCCUPANCY sampled at a phase boundary**, and a contested hex keeps its old value.

**MEASURED, and the difference is not academic.** Full 111-turn campaigns:

| instrument | peak contiguous Axis rail run | 54.41 gate |
|---|---|---|
| `GameState.control` (occupancy) | **3** (seed 1941), **4** (seed 4) | **never opens** |
| `GameState.rail_control` (54.41's own, sticky pass-through) | **9 / 9 / 18 / 20 / 18** | **opens in 5 of 5 seeds** |

Per-seed, with the faithful instrument (seeds 1941 / 7 / 4 / 24 / 2026):

| seed | rail hexes | Axis claims | Allied claims | peak run | peak turn | stages gate OPEN |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1941 | 66 | 17 | 65 | 9 | 6 | 140 / 332 (42%) |
| 7 | 66 | 18 | 61 | 9 | 5 | 134 / 332 (40%) |
| 4 | 68 | 23 | 69 | 18 | 7 | 160 / 332 (48%) |
| 24 | 66 | 21 | 62 | 20 | 19 | **298 / 332 (90%)** |
| 2026 | 66 | 20 | 66 | 18 | 5 | 154 / 332 (46%) |

**So 54.4 is NOT a marginal rule — its precondition holds for roughly HALF THE WAR**, and the old
instrument understated the Axis's rail control by 3–5x. Had this been built haul-first and measured
with `control`, the slice would have concluded "inert" and shelved a live rule. (Earlier geography
probes are in `scratchpad/54.4-scan/`: the railway spans r100–139, the Axis event log reaches r130,
and the Commonwealth holds 65 of 66 rail hexes by war's end — `axis_controlled_at_end` is 0 in every
seed, so the Axis takes the line mid-war and loses it again.)

## Built in this step (the CONTROL half only)

`game/rail.py` — 54.41 and nothing else: `rail_hexes`, `controlled_by`, `contiguous_runs`,
`longest_run`, `gate_open`, `claims`. `GameState.rail_control` (Coord -> Side) is a **separate field
from `control`**, for the reasons above. `EventKind.RAIL_CONTROL_CHANGED` + its fold in `apply`.

**`engine._traversed` is the one hook every mover now calls.** Rule 26's minefield encounter and
54.41's rail claim key off the hexes a unit CROSSED rather than the hex it ended in, and must see the
same path or they disagree about where the army has been. The six crossing sites (ordinary Movement
8.1, Reaction 8.51, the CP-free shuffle, the 26.25 vehicle case, Retreat Before Assault 13.21, and
the defender's forced retreat) all route through it, so a future third path-keyed rule is one edit
rather than a six-site hunt — and missing one of those is silent unfaithfulness, not a crash.

**Judgement calls, flagged not silent:**
1. The claim uses **`u.side`, not the caller's `side`** — on a forced retreat those differ, and 54.41
   asks who MADE the move, not who caused it.
2. **"land combat unit of any type" is read as `u.is_combat`** — a supply column or HQ running down
   the line takes no possession of it.
3. **Both sides claim**, not just the Axis. The rule is written from the Axis's side because he is
   the borrower, but it says "the LAST player", so the Commonwealth must be able to take its own line
   back — otherwise an Axis claim would be permanent. (Measured: the CW re-claims 61–69 times a war
   against the Axis's 17–23, which is exactly why `axis_controlled_at_end` is 0.)
4. **"Contiguous" is taken over ordinary hex adjacency**, not "connected along the line". 54.41 does
   not define it, and this is the plain reading; it cannot under-grant the Axis a lever the book
   gives him.

## STILL TO BUILD (54.43/54.44/54.45), now known to be reachable

Rolling Stock: 250 Stores + 100 Fuel, imported and brought to a controlled+operative rail hex, are
**consumed permanently** (54.45) and buy **300 tons per Operations Stage in one direction**; 900
tons-equivalent lifts one Stacking Point of units (54.44); all stock is **destroyed** the moment the
five-contiguous gate closes (54.45). Plus 54.46's inherited constraints (54.33 one commodity, 54.34's
one dead Operations Stage per calendar month, 54.35 no re-move in the same stage).

**OPEN, and the same question every Tier-2 item has raised:** whether any policy ever spends 250
Stores + 100 Fuel on rolling stock is a DOCTRINE decision the book does not supply. 22.3 was blocked
by unbuilt 21.6 towing; 56.3 delivers into a dump no Axis truck ever loads from. Do not invent a
doctrine to make the number move — flag it for Gate C.

## STEP 2 BUILT (54.43/54.45/54.34) — AND THE MEASURED VERDICT

`game/rail.py` gains the Rolling Stock layer; `engine._axis_rail` runs it in the Convoy Stage
(54.43's own "Rail movement occurs in the Convoy Stage"), ahead of the lorries. `GameState.
rolling_stock`, `ROLLING_STOCK_ACTIVATED` / `ROLLING_STOCK_DESTROYED`, and `RailActivateOrder` /
`RailHaulOrder` on the Policy seam.

**THE HAUL REUSES THE COMMONWEALTH'S `RAIL_HAULED` EVENT, which is exactly what [54.46] asks
("All rules concerning the movement of troops/supplies and the use of the railroad that apply to
the Commonwealth apply equally to the Axis"). The Event's own `side` says whose train it is.**

### A SEVENTH "correct machinery, no driver" — and this one is the COMMONWEALTH's
`RAIL_HAULED` already existed: declared in `events.py`, folded in `apply.py`, registered in
`invariants._CONSERVATION_KINDS`, gated by `supply.rail_reachable`, and covered by
`tests/test_rail.py`. **Nothing has ever emitted it.** `game/scenario.py:341` says so outright:
"the 54.3 rail HAUL driver was deferred at Step 4 (no engine phase fires RAIL_HAULED)". So the
**Commonwealth's own 54.3 inland rail distribution has never run in this engine**, and the Axis's
borrowed-railway haul is the first thing that ever fires it. NAMED, not folded into this slice:
54.3 is a different rule with its own value question, and it is a bigger structural fact than
anything 54.4 shows.

### A REAL BUG THE TESTS CAUGHT
`_rail_activate` did not check 54.41's gate, so the Axis could buy a locomotive with fewer than five
contiguous hexes — legal, useless, and destroyed by 54.45 the next Operations Stage, burning 250
Stores and 100 Fuel for nothing. **[54.41] is the chapeau of the whole rule** ("he MAY USE such rail
hexes to transport equipment and personnel") and putting a locomotive on the line is using it, so
activation is now gated.

### *** THE VERDICT: 54.4 IS FAITHFUL, REACHABLE AT THE GATE, AND BLOCKED AT THE PRICE ***

A/B over seeds 1941/7/4, rail doctrine live vs. an Axis that never issues a rail order: **zero
activations, zero hauls, and the two arms are BYTE-IDENTICAL** (same determinism signature, same
truck tonnage, same VP). Diagnosed precisely rather than guessed (seed 1941, 332 sampled stages):

| precondition | stages |
|---|---|
| 54.41 gate SHUT | 192 |
| gate OPEN | **140** |
| ...minus 54.34's dead stage | −46 |
| **usable** | **94** |
| no Axis dump on a controlled rail hex | **0** |
| Axis dump present but **cannot afford 250 Stores + 100 Fuel** | **94** |
| could have bought | **0** |

**MY OWN PREDICTION WAS WRONG.** I expected "no Axis dump ever stands on the captured line"; that
count is zero — there is always one. The blocker is the PRICE, and specifically one half of it:

| dump on controlled rail | max STORES ever | max FUEL ever |
|---|---|---|
| AX-Dump#2 | **0** | 389 |
| AX-Dump#3 | **0** | 417 |
| AX-Dump#5 | **0** | 387 |
| AX-Well-ElDaba | 0 | 0 |

**The Axis can afford 54.43's 100 Fuel four times over and can NEVER afford its 250 Stores — his
forward dumps hold literally zero Stores at every one of the 94 usable stages.** That is not a
doctrine failure; it is a faithful downstream consequence of **rule 51.0**, which makes Stores a
per-Game-Turn upkeep drawn wholly from a co-located dump (see the S7 slice): Stores that reach a
forward Axis dump are eaten by the units standing on it before they can ever accumulate to 250.

**NOT TUNED AWAY.** The doctrine buys one locomotive and only one, and is flagged as an opinion; it
is never given a way to conjure Stores onto the railway. A commander who genuinely wanted rail would
have to deliberately accumulate 250 Stores at a rail hex against a standing per-turn drain, and
whether that is ever worth 300 tons an Operations Stage is a Gate-C question, not a heuristic's.

**This is the THIRD Tier-2 item in a row whose value is gated by something other than its own
transcription** — 22.3 by unbuilt 21.6 towing, 56.3 by a relay that never stages out of Tobruk, and
now 54.4 by the DAK's structurally-zero forward Stores. All three are doctrine/adjacent-rule
questions, and they are accumulating behind Gate C rather than being individually fixable.
