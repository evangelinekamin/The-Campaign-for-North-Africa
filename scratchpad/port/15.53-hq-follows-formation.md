# [15.53] / [6.15] -- HQ-follows-its-formation

Owner approved 2026-08-03 ("yeah we should build that"). This file corrects the framing I gave her.

## IT IS NOT AN ARCHITECTURE GAP. THE MACHINERY IS ALREADY COMPLETE.

I reported [15.53] as blocked on an engine-shape decision -- "non-combat HQs cannot be attach
parents". That is wrong, and re-reading the code says so plainly:

- `organization.may_attach` (organization.py:282) has **no combat test at all**. It rejects on
  `formation(parent.org_type)`, self-attach, double-attach, hex mismatch and the [19.5] maxima.
  A non-combat HQ with an org_type row is a legal Parent today.
- `engine._movement` (engine.py:3837) **already carries the whole formation**: `_co_located_subtree`
  walks the tree transitively, the carried units cost no extra CP and no fuel, and the [8.37]
  stacking test at the destination already uses the FORMATION's footprint, not the bare HQ's
  (engine.py:3839-3842).

What is actually missing is a **driver**. This is instance #11 of "correct machinery, nobody ever
issues the order":

- No proposer anywhere orders a bare non-combat HQ to move (`campaign_policy.py:438`: "every
  proposer in this repo skips a non-combat unit").
- An attached subsidiary may not self-move ([19.12], engine.py:3792).
- So folding a formation into a non-combat HQ **freezes it where it stands** -- measured, and it
  killed the faucet: "the Eighth Army's Delta brigades concentrated and never reached the railhead"
  (campaign_policy.py:82-98).
- `CampaignPolicy.concentrate_formations` therefore gates concentration on `p.is_combat`
  (campaign_policy.py:120). That gate is a **workaround for the missing driver**, and its own
  docstring flags it as "an engine limitation, not a rule".

Consequence today, stated as the docstring does: EVERY German Parent Formation is a bare
non-combat HQ, so **the DAK concentrates nothing**, while the Italian binary divisions do. [15.53]
Organization Size bites for the Axis through its ITALIAN formations only -- the reverse of the
historical picture.

## THE PREREQUISITE IS [6.15], AND THE ENGINE ALREADY FLAGS IT

`_co_located_subtree`'s docstring (engine.py:3899-3904) flags that the mover's reach is gated on
the **Parent's** CPA, not the lowest in the formation, and declares the simplification immaterial
"while the only Parents that carry a subtree are homogeneous foot-infantry regiments -- a motorized
formation's HQ is non-combat and never carries".

**Opening the driver makes that flag material.** `data/unit_stats.json` prints `GE.hq` at
**CPA 60, MOTORIZED** and `CW.hq` at **CPA 30, MOTORIZED**. Without [6.15] a German HQ would drag
foot infantry across the desert at motorized speed.

[6.15] is verbatim (docs/rules/06-the-capability-point-system.md:24), and it names this very case:

> "The CPA of a "Parent Formation" (e.g., division, brigade, etc.) is that of the lowest CPA of the
> units comprising that parent formation, regardless of the CPA of any higher-CPA units. If the
> British 7th Armored Division was to move as an entity (consult the OA Sheet for the 7th Arm. Div)
> it would move with a CPA of '10', or that of its lowest-CPA unit -- the 1st KRRC (without trucks)."

Note [6.17]: a motorized infantry unit "assume[s] the CPA of the Truck unit carrying it", so the
minimum must be taken over EFFECTIVE CPA (post-motorization), not printed CPA.

## THE SLICE, IN ORDER

**Step 1 -- [6.15] lowest-CPA binding. Self-contained, faithful, lands alone.**
Three sites call `tactics.reachable_for_prev(r.state, u, ...)` with the bare Parent:
engine.py:3832 (movement), engine.py:3970 (continual), engine.py:6615. Compute the carried subtree
BEFORE the reach call and bind the mover's CPA to `min` over {parent} + subtree.
- THE CACHE HAZARD IS NOT REAL HERE, checked: `reachable_for_prev` (tactics.py:228) hands
  `zoc.reachable_with_zoc_prev` only primitives -- `state.terrain, unit.hex, budget, unit.mobility`
  and the frozensets. No Unit is ever a cache key, so the `replace(unit, ...)` landmine does not
  apply. **Do not synthesise a modified Parent counter.** Lower the BUDGET instead:
  `budget = _cp_ceiling(effective_cpa(...)) - unit.cp_used` at tactics.py:233 is the single line,
  and the formation's CPA is `min(effective_cpa(state, x) for x in [parent] + carried)`.
  `effective_cpa` (not printed cpa) is what [6.17]'s motorized-infantry clause requires.
- TERRAIN, AND THIS HALF IS A JUDGEMENT CALL -- FLAG IT. [6.15] speaks only of CPA, but a motorized
  HQ carrying foot infantry also raises "whose mobility class does the counter cross ground under?"
  `tactics.may_step_into(state, units, src, dst)` (tactics.py:258) already answers exactly this for
  forced relocations -- "may EVERY unit in `units` legally cross" -- so reusing it for a carried
  formation is consistent with existing practice. It is an INFERENCE, not printed text; say so.
- BLAST RADIUS, MEASURED not assumed (`scenario.campaign(1)`, 745 units, 98 with an org_type):
  there are **15 combat Parents**, and in EVERY ONE the Parent's own CPA already IS the formation
  minimum -- 10 Italian infantry regiments at 10/10, three Italian tank groups at 20/20,
  1-Army-Tank-Bde at 25/25, and IT-4-CCNN---(4CN) at parent 10 vs kids {10,15,25}. So [6.15]
  **lands byte-identical on the setup tree**, which is the ideal prerequisite: pure faithfulness,
  no measurement argument to have.
  BUT DO NOT CLAIM BYTE-IDENTICAL FROM THE SETUP ROSTER ALONE. In play a **guest** attaches under
  [19.5] without being assigned, and a guest may carry a LOWER CPA than its Parent (an artillery
  battalion at 15 joining a CPA-20 tank group drops the formation to 15). Determinism must be
  proven by running the panel, not by reading the setup.

**A SEPARATE LATENT BUG, FOUND WHILE CHECKING THIS -- it does not wait on the HQ work.**
The engine docstring says the current carriers are "homogeneous foot-infantry regiments". They are
not, quite: **IT-4-CCNN---(4CN) carries kids of mobility {FOOT, MOTORIZED} under a FOOT Parent**.
Since the counter crosses ground on the PARENT's `unit.mobility` (tactics.py:238), a motorized
subsidiary carried by a foot Parent today bypasses every mobility gate written against it --
[8.44]'s Salt Marsh ban and [8.45]'s desert gate among them. That is the mirror of the hazard the
HQ work introduces, it is live right now, and `tactics.may_step_into` is already the fix for both.

**Step 2 -- the driver.** Teach the campaign movement proposer to order Parent counters that carry a
subtree, so a concentrated formation can march. The DESTINATION choice is doctrine and belongs to
the staff; what is being fixed here is that a concentrated formation is currently IMMOBILE, which is
a defect, not a doctrine gap.

SHAPE, AND FOLLOW THE `_railway` PRECEDENT. `CampaignCommonwealthPolicy.movement`
(campaign_policy.py:434) already composes three sources, and its own comment states the invariant
that makes this safe: "an engineer is not a combat unit 'in any way, shape, or form' (23.11), and
every proposer in this repo skips a non-combat unit. So its orders simply ride alongside, and no
unit can be ordered twice." A `_formations(state, side)` proposer riding alongside `_railway` is
the same shape and the same guarantee.

REJECTED ALTERNATIVE, and worth writing down so it is not re-proposed: widening the proposers'
`u.is_combat` predicate to "is_combat OR carries a combat subtree". It is tempting because it
reuses every existing doctrine for free, but `is_combat` is read at eight sites in this file and
several of them mean COMBAT POWER, not "is a manoeuvre element" -- campaign_policy.py:839 builds
the enemy list as `u.is_combat and u.strength >= 1`, where a bare HQ genuinely does not belong.
Widening one predicate to mean two things is how a quiet defect gets in.

THE ENGINE ALREADY ACCEPTS THE ORDER. Checked: `engine._movement`'s rejection chain has no
`is_combat` test anywhere, so a MoveOrder naming a non-combat HQ is validated like any other. The
blocker really is only the proposer.

**Step 3 -- drop the `p.is_combat` gate** at campaign_policy.py:120 and rewrite that docstring. The
German and CW brigade/division tiers of [15.53] come alive.

**Step 4 -- measure.** A/B on the seed PANEL (CAMPAIGN_PANEL), never a single seed. The specific
question: does the DAK now concentrate, does [15.53] fire for the Germans, and does the Eighth
Army's faucet survive concentration (the failure that forced the gate in the first place)?

## WHAT COULD STILL GO WRONG

The measured failure mode -- concentrate, then freeze, then starve -- is a real risk if step 2's
proposer is weaker than the per-unit proposer it replaces. Step 3 must not land before step 2 is
shown to move formations, and the faucet check in step 4 is the specific guard against repeating it.
