1. THE MECHANISM -- how much does the Commonwealth actually recover under 22.3?
==============================================================================================

WHAT THE RULE RESTORES is broken-down TOE Strength Points (tanks, armoured cars, recce) and
broken-down Truck Points, so those are the units of account.

  OVER SEVEN 111-TURN WARS:
    Facility TOE points recovered ............ 54   (5 / 4 / 13 / 10 / 8 / 8 / 6 per war)
    Field    TOE points recovered ........... 1286
    Facility share of TOE recovery ........... 4.0%
    Facility TRUCK points recovered .......... 128  (ZERO in six wars; all 128 in seed 1)
    Field    TRUCK points recovered ......... 2689
    Facility share of truck recovery ......... 4.5%, and it is one seed, not a rate

  EVERY FACILITY REPAIR IN ALL SEVEN WARS WAS COMMONWEALTH.  The Axis got zero, in every war.

  WHERE.  Unit repairs: 51 points at axial r=132 and 3 at r=133 -- BOTH ALEXANDRIA HEXES AND
  NOTHING ELSE.  Truck repairs: all 128 at r=139, one Cairo hex, one seed.  Tobruk (r=66), a
  Major Facility for whoever holds it and the only one the Axis can reach on this map, saw
  ZERO repairs by either side in seven wars.

  WHO.  In six of seven seeds the SAME THREE COUNTERS take every facility repair in the war:
  2-RTR and 7-RTR on Game-Turn 5 and 3rd-Hussars-[7] on Game-Turn 6, at Alexandria.  Seed 4
  adds four more and names the pattern outright: 2-Armd-Div-Cruiser-Regt-I/II at GT27 and
  Tiger-Convoy:-Matilda at GT38 -- i.e. the next formations to ARRIVE in the Delta.  The
  Major Facility is reached by, and only by, the Eighth Army's rear echelon on its way out of
  the Delta.

  THE FACILITY COLUMN IS EXACTLY AS STRONG AS THE BOOK SAYS.  Of the points it was allowed to
  attempt it repaired 54/78 = 69% (units) and 128/226 = 57% (trucks); the Field column
  repaired 1286 of 77,412 offered point-attempts (1.7%) and 2689 of 243,084 (1.1%).  Per
  attempt the Major column is roughly forty times the Field one.  It simply almost never gets
  to see anything.

  [22.35] NEVER BIT.  Fuel spent 78 = Stores spent 78 = points offered 78 (units); 226 = 226
  = 226 (trucks).  Every single point ever presented to a Major Facility was funded: the
  Delta's dumps always paid.  The FIELD FALLBACK the review repair added (correctly -- 22.22
  grants Field Repair in "any hex") therefore never fired once in a campaign, in seven wars.
  It is right, and it is inert here.

  [22.34a] IS UNREACHABLE IN THE CAMPAIGN.  No Major-Facility hex ever loses a Fortification
  Level in a whole war (Alexandria/Cairo stay at 3, Tobruk at 2, measured on seeds 1941/1/4).
  The modifier never fires, and with it the [22.8] Major column's die-7 (25%) and die-8 (10%)
  rows are never reached -- transcribed-but-unreachable, the same shape as the Temporary
  column.  Nothing to fix; the modifier is correct and its trigger (bombing/barrage of the
  Delta) is a thing this campaign does not do.

WHY SO LITTLE -- THE PRECISE DIAGNOSIS (scratchpad/gate223/GEO.json)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
21.6 TOWING does not exist in this engine (named debt in game/repair.py).  A broken vehicle
can therefore only ever use a facility if it is STANDING ON ONE when the Repair Phase comes
round.  Measured, for every broken point in the war, by hex distance to the nearest of the
eight Major-Facility hexes:

  Commonwealth TOE, seed 1941 / 1 / 4:
    on a facility hex (d0) ......  2 /  2 / 11        (0.8% / 0.5% / 3.8% of what broke)
    1-5 hexes away .............. 77 / 99 / 72
    6+ hexes away .............. 170 /300 /203        (68% / 75% / 71%)
  AXIS TOE, all three seeds:  ONE HUNDRED PERCENT at 6+ hexes.  Not one Axis TOE point broke
    within five hexes of a facility in any war -- which is the whole of why the Axis scored
    zero facility repairs.  The DAK is never near Alexandria or Cairo, and Tobruk is a hex it
    drives through, not one it breaks down on.
  TRUCKS: 100% at 6+ hexes in seeds 1941 and 4.  Seed 1 is the exception that proves it --
    20 points broke ON a Cairo hex and 44 two hexes away, and that one lingering Commonwealth
    truck park is the entire 128-point truck facility total of this gate.

  THE PORT PLAN'S OWN VERDICT ON THIS ITEM WAS "HIGH -- this is the Commonwealth's entire
  rear-area recovery economy" (00-THE-PORT-PLAN.md:1146).  MEASURED, IT IS NOT AN ECONOMY.
  It is a 4%-of-recovery side channel, and the reason is nameable in one line: the book's own
  delivery mechanism for a facility -- 21.6 towing -- is unbuilt, so the facility can only
  serve what happens to break down on top of it.  22.3 is not mis-built; it is starved of
  input by a different unbuilt rule.

2. DOES IT REACH THE FRONT?
==============================================================================================
YES -- ALL OF IT, IN EVERY SEED, AND FOR A STRUCTURAL REASON.

Every counter that took a facility repair subsequently marched WEST out of the Delta:

  seed   counters  points   r at repair -> best r reached   moved   west of Alexandria
  1941      3         5         132 -> 76                    3/3      5/5 points
     7      3         4         132 -> 93                    3/3      4/4
     4      7        13         132 -> 85                    7/7     13/13
    24      3        10         132 -> 85                    3/3     10/10
  2026      3         8         132 -> 94                    3/3      8/8
    99      3         8         132 -> 94                    3/3      8/8
     1      3         6         132 -> 77                    3/3      6/6
     1  (trucks) 3   128        139 -> 86                    3/3    128/128 points

  Median advance after repair: 38-56 hexes in six seeds, 6 hexes in seed 2026 (whose median
  counter stopped at El Hamman while its best reached r=94).  ALL SEVEN seeds put repaired
  counters west of Mersa Matruh (r<100); four put them west of Sidi Barrani (r<86); one
  (1941) put two of three west of Bardia (r<77).  NONE ever got west of Tobruk (r<66) -- the
  Axis rout holds them east of it, which is a fact about the campaign, not about 22.3.

  THE REASON IT REACHES THE FRONT IS THE SAME REASON THERE IS SO LITTLE OF IT.  The only
  vehicles a facility can serve are ones already moving through the Delta toward the front;
  Alexandria is the last facility hex on the way out.  The rule catches the Eighth Army's
  armour mid-march, mends it, and the march continues.  There is no "recovered in the rear and
  stranded there" failure mode to report -- the geometry forbids it.

  ONE UNFLAGGED DEBT THIS QUESTION EXPOSES -- [22.5], and it is exactly about reaching the
  front.  Scan-verified, PDF p.035: "Repaired vehicles are treated as REPLACEMENT POINTS for
  that type of unit.  See Case 20.2. ... they follow all the rules laid down for Replacement
  Points, except that they do not have to undergo Training."  This engine instead heals the
  counter that broke, in place (apply.py:518-521, which cites 22.5 for behaviour 22.5 does not
  describe).  The pool the rule names ALREADY EXISTS in state -- replacement_pool,
  credit_replacements, replacements_available, and the replacement_training ledger 22.5
  explicitly says to skip.  While field repair was the only repair, in-place healing and the
  pool coincided (the vehicle is repaired where its parent stands).  22.3 breaks that
  coincidence: under the book, materiel repaired in Cairo becomes a pooled Tank/Recce
  Replacement Point that 20.2 can allot to ANY eligible unit and ship forward, which is a
  second, larger route to the front that this engine does not have.  22.5 is not in
  game/repair.py's NAMED DEBT list.  It should be, and it is the natural next slice on this
  rule -- smaller than 21.6 towing and it needs no new state.

4. SANITY
==============================================================================================
  * NO ABSURDITIES in any arm, any seed.  Repaired points never exceeded broken points within
    a run (checked per class per side, in-process); no counter ended with negative
    broken_down; no repair landed on a counter absent from the final state; game.invariants
    never raised.
  * NO RESURRECTION.  Repair only ever reduces an existing counter's broken_down; nothing
    re-raises a dead unit or a destroyed truck formation.
  * NO RUNAWAY.  Largest single repair: 3 TOE points, and 9 Truck Points -- the latter only in
    seed 1 and only on the facility path, which is correct and is the [22.34] percentage
    column's own signature (the Field truck column is capped at 2 by the chart).
  * TERMINATION.  All 21 campaigns completed; 429-552 s each, no outliers.
  * ONE PRE-EXISTING FLAG FOUND WHILE READING THE CHART FOR THIS GATE, out of scope and
    affecting BOTH arms identically: the [22.8] Field Truck cells are CODES, not point counts
    -- the chart's own legend reads "1 = One Light or Medium Truck Point is repaired; 2 = One
    HEAVY Truck Point (or any combination of Medium and Light Truck Points equal to two)".
    combat_tables._FIELD_REPAIR returns a flat 2/1 and engine._field_repair_trucks spends it
    without consulting TruckFormation.truck_class, so a die of 0/1 repairs TWO points of a
    HEAVY formation where the book repairs one.  The campaign fields 1 Axis and 3 Commonwealth
    heavy formations, so the error is small and two-sided.  It predates 22.3 (the 21.11/22.23
    pass) and is NOT this gate's to fix; recorded so it is not rediscovered.
  * CHECKED AND INERT, not a defect: [22.14] grants the Commonwealth "may repair all his
    vehicles together" while the Axis must split German from Italian, and engine._repair keys
    the AC/Recce pool on nationality for BOTH sides -- but every Commonwealth counter in this
    order of battle carries the single nationality label "CW" (410 of 410), so the key never
    splits the Commonwealth.  Axis is IT 230 / GE 105, which is the split the rule wants.
  * CHECKED AND INERT: the OPEN OWNER RULING f3fa0a8 records (22.31 qualifies Tobruk "for
    whoever controls it" and nothing enforces the qualifier) is inert at campaign scale too --
    ZERO repairs occurred at Tobruk for either side across seven full wars, so no controller
    question was ever put.
  * BENIGN, RECORDED: engine._prepay_repairs always attempts the MAXIMUM number of points the
    hex can fund.  That is optimal under a percentage column and free at Alexandria/Cairo (the
    rule-57 base), so it costs nothing today; it is a decision the book leaves to the Player
    ("he must decide how many attempts he will make", 22.42's wording for the destroyed-tank
    case) and would want a policy hook the day a facility exists somewhere supply is scarce.

APPENDIX -- THE NEUTER AND INSTRUMENT PROOFS, IN FULL
==============================================================================================
A. THE READ-ONLY INSTRUMENT IS INERT.  gate223_ab.py wraps engine._facility_repair_units /
   _facility_repair_trucks / _field_repair_units / _field_repair_trucks with pure delegating
   recorders, so the column a repaired point came off is THE FUNCTION THE ENGINE CALLED and
   not a heuristic over which commodities were spent.  Proved inert by running the same seed
   with the wrappers installed and uninstalled (--instrument on|off), campaign(1941) truncated
   to 12 turns:
       instrument on   sig 2cfb3f2cf005f55a      (inert_instrument_on_12turn.json)
       instrument off  sig 2cfb3f2cf005f55a      (inert_instrument_off_12turn.json)
   Byte-identical.  Truncation is legitimate here: a wrapper is either inert or it is not, and
   that does not depend on turn count.

B. THE NEUTER REACHES.  game/engine.py does "from . import ... repair ..." (a MODULE import),
   so repair.major_facility_hexes is an attribute lookup at call time and rebinding
   game.repair.major_facility_hexes reaches engine._repair.  This is the trap tests/baselines.py
   records (patching a symbol the caller did not resolve through), and it is the reason the
   patch goes on the module attribute rather than on a from-imported name.
       noop          sig 2cfb3f2cf005f55a      identical to live -- the machinery is inert
       facility_off  sig e6f529e37e0b40b5      differs -- the switch has consequence
   and at FULL length, in all seven wars, the facility_off arm entered the instrumented
   facility path ZERO times (paths facility/unit = facility/truck = 0 calls) while live
   entered it 39 + 69 times.  The in-process assertions (_assert_neuter_reached) would have
   killed the run rather than report a number had either failed.
